# Sales & Business Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes 10,000+ retail sales records to identify trends in sales, profitability, regional performance, product performance, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

The project uses Excel, SQL, and Power BI to perform data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and business insight generation.

💡 Key Insights
Technology generated the highest overall sales and profit among the three categories.
The West region recorded the highest sales.
Sales increased substantially from 2024 through 2026.
Several higher discount levels were associated with negative aggregated profit.
The products generating the highest sales were not always the same products generating the highest profit.

🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights that could support decisions related to product performance, regional performance, profitability, and discount strategy.
